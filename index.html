Our Story

"From a chance coffee shop encounter to a lifetime of adventures together."

2019 — The First Meeting

Sarah and Michael first met at a cozy cafe in Manila while both reaching for the last matcha latte. A conversation started over shared favorite books, and they haven't stopped talking since.

2022 — Welcoming Milo

After three years of shared travels, late-night dinners, and countless coffee dates, they adopted their beloved rescue golden retriever, Milo, who brought endless joy into their home.

2025 — The Proposal

During a breathtaking sunset walk along the beach in El Nido, Michael popped the question. Surrounded by the gentle ocean waves, Sarah gave a resounding "Yes!"
